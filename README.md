# Khibra

**Khibra** is a curated, managed on-demand expert marketplace designed for the Saudi market, connecting organizations with vetted senior-level independent experts for advisory, fractional executive, mentoring, and project-based engagements.

Unlike open freelance platforms, Khibra follows a **curated and managed marketplace model** where experts are carefully vetted and engagements are actively matched and supported throughout their lifecycle.

---

## About Khibra

Saudi organizations undergoing transformation often require short-term access to deep, senior-level expertise without the cost, time, or commitment associated with permanent senior hires or traditional consulting firms.

At the same time, Saudi Arabia has a growing pool of highly experienced senior professionals whose expertise can continue to create significant value beyond full-time employment.

Khibra bridges this gap by providing organizations with trusted access to senior expertise through a structured, flexible, and professionally managed platform.

---

## Our Solution

Khibra provides a managed marketplace that:

- Sources and rigorously vets senior experts.
- Maintains retired senior professionals as the primary expert segment.
- Selectively includes non-retired senior professionals to address specific capability gaps.
- Matches organizations with suitable experts based on their engagement needs.
- Supports the full engagement lifecycle from request and matching through contracting, delivery, payment, and review.
- Positions experts based on their expertise, credibility, track record, and business impact.

---

## Business Objectives

Khibra aims to:

- Build a trusted and curated pool of senior Saudi and Saudi-market-relevant experts.
- Provide organizations with a faster, lower-risk, and more flexible alternative to traditional consulting and permanent senior hiring.
- Establish a credible, premium brand for senior expertise.
- Create a repeatable and manageable engagement lifecycle.
- Develop a sustainable platform revenue model.
- Support Saudization and structured knowledge transfer.
- Maintain a controlled non-retired expert segment for specific capability and supply gaps.

## Engagement Types

Khibra supports four primary engagement models:

### Advisory
Ongoing or periodic strategic guidance from an experienced senior expert.

### Fractional Executive
A senior professional serves in a recurring, part-time leadership capacity without becoming a full-time executive.

### Mentoring
Structured knowledge-transfer engagements focused on developing the client's internal talent.

### Project-Based
Defined-scope and defined-duration engagements designed to deliver a specific outcome or deliverable.

## How Khibra Works

The platform supports a structured engagement lifecycle:

**Client Request → Expert Matching → Proposal → Contract → Delivery → Payment → Review**

1. Experts apply to join Khibra.
2. Applications are reviewed through a curated vetting process.
3. Approved experts create and maintain their professional profiles and availability.
4. Client organizations submit an **Engagement Brief** describing their requirements.
5. Khibra identifies and presents suitable experts.
6. The client and expert agree on scope, timeline, and commercial terms.
7. The engagement is formally contracted.
8. Progress, milestones, and deliverables are tracked.
9. Payment and expert payout are processed according to the agreed terms.
10. Both parties can provide feedback after completion.

## Product Principles

### Curated by Design

Khibra is **not an open freelancer marketplace**. Experts are vetted and approved before becoming available for engagements.

### Expertise Over Demographics

Client-facing profiles, matching, search, and platform content focus on:

- Expertise
- Professional experience
- Achievements
- Credibility
- Business outcomes

Age and retirement status are not intended to be used as client-facing value propositions.

### Managed Engagements

Khibra supports the engagement beyond discovery by providing a structured process for matching, proposals, contracting, delivery, payments, and feedback.

### Knowledge Transfer

The platform enables organizations to benefit not only from expert delivery but also from structured mentoring and capability transfer to internal teams.
